Mission
Mission

Our mission is to guide churches and leaders to the right fit for ministry.

We combine timeless biblical wisdom with leading-edge technologies, proven assessment methodologies, and feature-rich tools to empower you with clarity and confidence.

01

Clarity in Calling. Confidence in Leadership.

For over 40 years, MinistryMatch has helped pastors, leaders, and churches experience greater clarity in their calling and confidence in their ministry decisions. Built on biblical principles and decades of research, MinistryMatch combines spiritual insight with data-driven precision to help people and churches serve where they are most effective.

A proven process

At the heart of MinistryMatch is a commitment to align people and churches according to their God-given design.

Through a suite of proven tools, assessments, and guided processes, we help individuals discover their unique calling, churches clarify what kind of leader they need, and denominations strengthen leadership alignment across their network.

You care deeply about finding the right fit for ministry—we do too, and that’s exactly what MinistryMatch is designed to help you do.
